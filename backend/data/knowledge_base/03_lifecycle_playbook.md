# ABL Lifecycle Playbook

## Stage 01 — Origination & Prospecting
The relationship manager screens the prospect's industry, revenue scale, and collateral mix (accounts receivable and inventory-heavy businesses are the natural ABL fit). A preliminary borrowing base is modeled from the prospect's most recent AR aging and inventory summary to produce an indicative advance-rate range and facility size, which is captured in a term sheet. Term sheets are non-binding and subject to full underwriting and field exam findings.

## Stage 02 — Underwriting & Credit Approval
The underwriting team spreads three years of financial statements, analyzes collateral quality and customer concentration in the AR portfolio, reviews industry and management risk, and assigns a preliminary risk rating. Findings are documented in a credit approval memo that goes to the credit officer or loan committee, with authority level determined by facility size and risk rating per the bank's delegated authority matrix.

## Stage 03 — Structuring & Documentation
Legal and credit negotiate the credit agreement: advance rates, the ineligibles definition, reserves, financial covenants (including the springing FCCR trigger), reporting requirements, and — where a term lender or FILO tranche is also in the capital structure — the intercreditor agreement governing lien priority and standstill rights.

## Stage 04 — Closing & Funding
Conditions precedent (corporate authority documents, insurance certificates, landlord waivers, UCC lien searches) are cleared, UCC-1 financing statements are filed and confirmed, and the facility is initially funded against the closing-date borrowing base.

## Stage 05 — Boarding & Onboarding
Loan operations sets up the facility in the servicing system, configures the borrowing base certificate template to match the negotiated formula and reserves, and establishes the covenant testing calendar and reporting due dates.

## Stage 06 — Servicing & Collateral Monitoring (recurring)
The borrower submits a Borrowing Base Certificate on the agreed cadence (weekly is typical for tighter-availability credits, monthly for well-collateralized ones). Each submission is checked against the eligibility rules, availability is recalculated, dilution and concentration trends are tracked period over period, and cash dominion status is confirmed against the excess-availability trigger.

## Stage 07 — Field Exams & Appraisals (recurring)
Field exams are scheduled on a risk-based cadence — more frequent when average excess availability is low or the risk rating has weakened. Exams test AR aging accuracy, inventory existence and valuation, and the borrower's internal reporting controls; exceptions found (for example, previously unreported ineligibles) typically result in an immediate borrowing base adjustment. Inventory NOLV appraisals are refreshed on a similar risk-based schedule, typically at least annually.

## Stage 08 — Covenant Compliance & Financial Reporting (recurring)
Borrower financial statements are ingested on the agreed reporting cadence (monthly or quarterly) and covenants are tested — most importantly the springing FCCR, which is only calculated when the excess-availability trigger has been breached during the period. A compliance certificate documents the test results; a breach requires escalation for waiver, amendment, or default remedy consideration.

## Stage 09 — Portfolio & Risk Monitoring (recurring)
Risk ratings are reviewed on a regular schedule and immediately upon a trigger event. Early-warning indicators — declining availability trends, rising dilution, industry stress signals, concentration creep — feed into watchlist recommendations. Portfolio-level views track concentration by industry, risk rating, and single-obligor exposure.

## Stage 10 — Renewal, Amendment & Annual Review
Ahead of facility maturity (or on the credit's annual review date), the deal team refreshes the full underwriting analysis, re-tests advance rates against updated field exam and appraisal findings, and either renews, amends, or begins an orderly maturity/refinance conversation. Waivers and amendments outside the annual cycle follow the same authority matrix as new credit approval, scaled to materiality.

## Workout Branch — Special Assets, Default & Workout
Triggered by a material, uncured covenant breach, a sustained overadvance, or a severe risk-rating downgrade. The credit transfers to the special assets function, which manages forbearance negotiations, restructuring options, and — where recovery requires it — an orderly liquidation of collateral, coordinated with legal and, where applicable, other lenders under the intercreditor agreement.

## Governance Note
Every stage above produces a decision or a recommendation that is subject to human approval at the authority level the bank's delegated authority matrix assigns to it. No stage in this playbook is intended to run to completion without a human decision-maker at its approval gate.
